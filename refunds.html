Refund Policy

LAST UPDATED · MAY 2026

Refund request process

Refunds at Mindshift Labs are not automatic. Each request is individually reviewed and evaluated by our team. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval. We are committed to being fair, but we reserve the right to decline requests that do not meet our criteria.

How to submit a request

To initiate a refund review, email support@mindshiftlabs.lat with the subject line "Refund Request". Your email must include:

  • The email address used to purchase
  • Date of purchase
  • A detailed explanation of why you are requesting a refund
  • Whether you accessed the PDFs and/or used the app

Review criteria

Our team evaluates each request based on the following factors:

  • Time elapsed since purchase (requests made within 7 days are given priority consideration)
  • Extent of product usage (PDFs downloaded, app sessions, features accessed)
  • Nature of the reason provided
  • Whether additional information is required from the customer

Our team may request additional information or evidence before making a decision. Failure to respond within 5 business days will result in the request being closed.

Review timeline

All refund requests are reviewed within 5–10 business days of receiving all required information. You will receive a written decision via email. If approved, the refund is processed within 5 business days through Paddle's payment platform.

Non-refundable situations

Refund requests will generally not be approved in the following cases:

  • PDFs have been downloaded and the content has been accessed in full
  • More than 14 days have passed since the purchase date
  • The request is for a monthly subscription charge ($9/mo) beyond the first billing cycle
  • The account has been used actively for more than 7 days in the app

Subscription charges

Monthly subscription charges ($9/mo, active from day 31) are subject to the same review process. To avoid future charges, cancel your subscription from inside the app before the next billing date. Cancellation requests do not automatically trigger a refund of the current period.
